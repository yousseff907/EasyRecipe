About EasyRecipe

The Story

EasyRecipe was built by a student at 42 Beirut out of a simple frustration - watching cooking videos on TikTok and Instagram, pausing every few seconds to write down ingredients, rewinding to catch a step. There had to be a better way.

So this became a project: paste a link, get a full recipe. No ads, no paywalls, no accounts required to view shared recipes. Just a tool that does one thing well.

What EasyRecipe Does

  • Extracts recipes from TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook cooking videos using AI
  • Supports photo slideshows - not just videos
  • Provides estimated nutritional information for every recipe
  • Lets you create and save your own recipes manually
  • Generates recipes from ingredients you already have at home
  • Lets you share recipes publicly with anyone via a simple link
  • Lets you discover and save recipes from other users

Features

Video & Slideshow Extraction

Create, save, and edit your own recipes with full control
