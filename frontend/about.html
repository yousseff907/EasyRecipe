About EasyRecipe

Our Mission

EasyRecipe transforms cooking videos from social media into organized, easy-to-follow recipes. We use AI to extract recipes from TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook videos, making it simple to save and cook your favorite dishes.

What We Do

  • Extract recipes from social media cooking videos using AI
  • Provide nutritional information for every recipe
  • Let you create and save your own recipes
  • Generate recipes from ingredients you have at home
  • Organize your personal recipe collection

Features

Video Extraction

Extract recipes from TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook instantly

AI Recipe Builder

Generate recipes from ingredients you have

Nutrition Facts

Get estimated nutritional information
